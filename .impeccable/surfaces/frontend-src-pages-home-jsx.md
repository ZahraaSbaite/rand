# Surface brief: storefront (Home + shared chrome)

Scope: Home page plus site-wide chrome (theme tokens, Navbar, Footer, ProductCard). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: mixed (trend shoppers, gift buyers, craft lovers), mostly browsing on phones. Job: find a piece they love, trust it is handmade, add it to the basket or start a commission.
Constraints: keep RRAND name and the light/dark toggle; all content and features stay; no invented claims (no fake reviews, counts, skill ratings).
Memorable moment: a single yarn strand that is worked down the page as you scroll, and pieces that drop into the basket as a rolling yarn ball.

## Direction contract

THESIS: RRAND's shop is a stack of 1970s crochet pattern leaflets: every piece is presented with its leaflet number, its facts and its making rows. It refuses the soft pastel boutique hero-over-card-grid.

OWN-WORLD: Four offset inks only: tomato (reserved for the buy action), marigold, avocado, chocolate, plus uncoated stock; flat full-width ink fields own whole sections. Shrikhand heavy italic for display, Archivo for text, set like pattern notation (Rnd 1:, ch, sl st). Rounded photo frames, dotted cut lines, stitch-marker badges. Night print: chocolate stock with the same inks.

STORY: Visitors see real pieces first, understand each is made by hand in small batches through the making rows, then add to basket or commission.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Marigold field. Leaflet header strip (RRAND · Pattern Leaflets · leaflet number). RRAND at display scale left, tagline, one line of positioning; right, the newest piece in a rounded photo frame with name, price and a working tomato Add to basket. A yarn strand starts here.

FORM: Pattern Leaflet, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 8e378f02. Signature interaction: scroll-drawn yarn strand plus yarn-ball fly-to-basket. Motion grammar: draw, stamp, roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
